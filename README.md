Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Word, First Thought**: send exactly one
word, the first that comes to mind, and let a judge compare it two at a time
against the field on surprise, vividness, and a spark of wit.

The chosen word is **defenestration** — the act of throwing someone out of a
window. It is surprising (nobody expects it as a first thought), vivid (it
conjures a whole scene in a single word), and witty (the absurd delight that
such a specific act has a name of its own, with a sound that rewards a second
look). `main.py` writes that one word to `/output/entry.txt`, falling back to
`./output/entry.txt` when `/output` is not writable.
